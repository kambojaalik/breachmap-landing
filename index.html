Project Roadmap

Three Planned Core Capabilities

BreachMap AI is designed around three foundational research pillars aimed at turning abstract security alerts into coherent, actionable topology models.

Interactive Attack-Path Visualization

Transforming fragmented cloud configs, network rules, and identity policies into navigable, multi-dimensional graph topologies that uncover lateral movement risks.

  • Full graph representation of multi-hop attack paths
  • Blast-radius modeling across identity and microservices
  • Clear distinction between isolated and chained vulnerabilities

AI-Assisted Security Analysis

Contextual AI reasoning that synthesizes technical vulnerability findings into plain-language causal explanations, cutting through alert fatigue and false positives.

  • Translates cryptic CVE telemetry into narrative risk contexts
  • Grounds threat analysis strictly in graph relationships
  • Identifies compound threat combinations undetectable in isolation

Risk Prioritization & Defensive Guidance

Targeted remediation recommendations focused on strategic choke points—stopping attacks at the lowest operational cost before they reach critical crown-jewel data.

  • Pinpoints minimal intervention points with maximum risk mitigation
  • Step-by-step hardening recipes for cloud engineering teams
  • Eliminates low-priority noise to focus defender bandwidth
Our Vision

Making Complex Security Relationships Intuitive and Understandable.

Modern enterprise architectures span across multiple cloud providers, hundreds of IAM permissions, ephemeral Kubernetes pods, and complex network configurations. The core problem facing security teams today is not a lack of data—it is a lack of coherent relational context.

Traditional scanners flood engineering teams with disconnected spreadsheets of CVE severities. However, real-world breaches rarely occur from an isolated high-scoring vulnerability; they succeed through an inconspicuous chain of minor misconfigurations and transitive permissions.

BreachMap AI is founded on the conviction that security should be visible, navigable, and intelligible. By marrying graph-theoretic topology with transparent language models, we aim to help teams visualize systemic risk long before it turns into an incident.

The Traditional Approach

Disjointed Alert Lists & Siloed Scanners

Hundreds of unranked vulnerability tickets with no structural context. Teams waste time patching isolated bugs on non-sensitive assets while subtle lateral movement paths remain unaddressed.

The BreachMap AI Vision

Connected Topologies & Explainable Choke Points

Interactive attack graph maps highlighting complete multi-hop chains. Clear identification of single remediation choke points that simultaneously break multiple threat pathways.
